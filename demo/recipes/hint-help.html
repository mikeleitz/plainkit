Status

Status is a short fact on the item card. Open, hold, and blocked are examples. The circle on the card is only a door into this page.

Hold

A hold pauses work. It is not an error. The warn circle on the card points here so the card itself can stay short.

Blocked

A block means the item cannot move until someone clears it. Use a banner when the whole page failed. Use this circle when the item is still on the page and the long story lives here.

Plan

Plan is a read-only fact sheet. A hint may sit after a label when the card has no toolbar title.
